Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

AYFM ("the app", "we", "us") is a reminder app that nags you to answer messages until you've replied. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information.

The short version

AYFM does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data. There is no account, no login, and no server. Everything you put into the app stays on your device.

What's stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, inside iOS's app container, so it can remind you:

  • Your reminders — the sender name, an optional note about the message, the source app name, when it was flagged, and how often to nag you.
  • Your settings — reminder intervals, widget layout, pause state, and names you've removed from quick-fill suggestions.

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is not backed up to any server we control.

Network use

The app makes only one kind of network request, and it does not send your personal data:

  • App icons. To show a recognizable logo for an app (e.g. WhatsApp), AYFM looks up that app's icon using Apple's public iTunes Search API. The request contains only the public app name — never your messages, contacts, or any personal information. Downloaded icons are cached on your device.

What we do not do

  • We do not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or the Advertising Identifier (IDFA).
  • We include no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • We do not run a backend, and we do not sell or share your information with anyone.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are generated locally on your device. No notification content is sent to or through any server.

Data retention and deletion

Because all data lives on your device, deleting the app removes all of it. There is no server-side copy for us to retain or delete.

Children

AYFM is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with a new effective date.
