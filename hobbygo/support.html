Support

HobbyGo! is a hobby journal that only ever celebrates that you showed up. There are no streaks and no "missed days" — a hobby you haven't done in a while just sits quietly and waits. This page covers how it works and how to get help.

Contact

Email kud3t4@proton.me for bug reports, feature requests, or anything else. We read every message and try to reply within a few days.

How it works

  • Add a hobby from the hobbies tab — give it a name, an emoji and a genre. The genre decides its color, so hobbies of the same kind read as a family.
  • Log an entry every time you do it. You can add a note about how it went and attach a photo — either tap take photo to snap one right then, or pick an existing shot from your photo library. Photos are optional; an entry works fine without one.
  • Each hobby's bar grows with every entry. Bars are scaled so the tallest is always one short of full — there's always room to grow.
  • Switch between week, month, year and all-time to see different stretches of time.

Goals and levels

  • A practice goal levels you up by showing up — do the hobby a set number of times and it counts itself.
  • A milestone goal is a named thing you're working toward ("learn Stairway", "read Dune"). It isn't counted: you tag the entries you spend on it and mark it done whenever you decide.
  • Either way, finishing a goal bumps that hobby up a level.
  • Goals can have an optional deadline, which shows a gentle countdown — nothing bad happens if it passes.

Other things it does

  • Calendar — see which hobbies you did on which days, with rest days marked as rest days.
  • Punch card — a count-down card you punch each time you do a hobby.
  • Hobby wheel — can't decide what to do? Spin it.
  • Retiring a hobby — hobbies you've moved on from go to the retired shelf under Settings instead of being deleted, so their history is kept.
  • Genres — rename, recolor, hide the built-in genres or add your own under Settings → genres.

Frequently asked questions

Does it nag me if I skip a day?

No. HobbyGo! sends no notifications at all and has no concept of a missed day. It only records the times you did something.

The camera won't open when I tap "take photo".

Check that camera access is allowed under iOS Settings → HobbyGo! → Camera. If you declined the prompt the first time, that's where you turn it back on. You can always attach a photo from your library instead — and the camera button doesn't appear at all on devices without a camera, such as the iOS Simulator.

Can I change a hobby's color?

Yes. A hobby uses its genre's color by default, and you can override it per hobby. To change a whole family at once, edit the genre under Settings → genres.

I deleted an entry by mistake.

Deleting an entry is permanent — there's no undo and no server-side copy. You can always log it again with the original date.

Is my data backed up or synced between devices?

Everything is stored locally on your device only. There is no account and no sync, so data doesn't move between devices.

How do I remove all my data?

Delete the app. All data is stored on your device, so removing the app removes everything.

Privacy

HobbyGo! collects no personal data — everything stays on your device, photos included. See our Privacy Policy.
