Support

Innerleaf keeps your physical notebooks with you: scan the pages, flip through them like a real book, and keep a running list of what you want to add next time you're actually holding the notebook. This page covers how it works and how to get help.

Contact

Email kud3t4@proton.me for bug reports, feature requests, or anything else. We read every message and try to reply within a few days.

How it works

  • The shelf is your home screen — every notebook you've added, with its cover and page count. Tap + to add a new one: give it a name and pick a cover color.
  • Scan pages with the camera. Innerleaf uses Apple's document scanner, so it finds the page edges and straightens the shot for you.
  • Flip through a notebook in the reader, with a page-turn animation instead of a photo grid.
  • Written pages. If there's something you'd rather type than scan, add a page with a title and body text — it sits in the notebook alongside the scans.
  • To add. Jot down anything you want to write into the real notebook later. Notes can be attached to a specific page or to the notebook as a whole, and you tick them off once you've written them in.

Covers

Long-press a notebook on the shelf to edit its name and color, set a cover image from your photos, or remove that image to go back to the plain color cover.

Frequently asked questions

Scanning isn't available — why?

Document scanning needs a real camera, so it isn't available in the iOS Simulator. On a device, check that camera access is allowed under iOS Settings → Innerleaf → Camera. You can always import pages from your photo library instead.

Can I reorder or delete pages?

Yes — pages keep a reading order you can change, and deleting a page removes it from the notebook. Deleting a notebook removes its pages and its to-add notes with it.

Does it read the handwriting on my pages?

No. Pages are stored as images. Nothing is transcribed and nothing is sent anywhere for processing.

Is my data synced or backed up?

Everything is stored locally on your device only. There is no account and no sync, so notebooks don't move between devices.

How do I remove all my data?

Delete the app. All data is stored on your device, so removing the app removes everything.

Privacy

Innerleaf collects no personal data — your notebooks stay on your device. See our Privacy Policy.
