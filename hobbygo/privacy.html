Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

HobbyGo! ("the app", "we", "us") is a no-pressure hobby journal — it records the times you did a hobby and never counts the times you didn't. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information.

The short version

HobbyGo! does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data. There is no account, no login, and no server. Everything you put into the app stays on your device.

What's stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, inside iOS's app container:

  • Your hobbies — name, emoji, genre, color, level and retired state.
  • Your entries — the date you logged a hobby, an optional note, an optional photo, and the goal it was tagged toward.
  • Your goals — what you're working toward, targets, optional deadlines, and which ones you've finished.
  • Your settings — genres and their colors, grouping and ordering preferences, font theme, and whether you've seen the intro.

Photos you attach are copied into the app's own private storage on your device. This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is not backed up to any server we control.

Camera

When you log an entry you can tap take photo to snap a picture on the spot. The app asks for camera access the first time you do, and the camera is used only while you're actively taking that photo. The picture is saved straight into your entry on your device and is never uploaded. If you'd rather not grant camera access, you can still attach photos from your library, or skip photos entirely.

Photos

When you attach an existing photo to an entry, iOS's photo picker lets you choose a single image. The app only ever receives the picture you pick — it has no access to the rest of your photo library, and it does not read location or other metadata from your library.

Photos taken in the app and photos you pick are both copied into the app's own private storage on your device. Neither is written back to your photo library.

Network use

HobbyGo! makes no network requests. It works entirely offline — photos included.

What we do not do

  • We do not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or the Advertising Identifier (IDFA).
  • We include no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • We do not run a backend, and we do not sell or share your information with anyone.

Data retention and deletion

Because all data lives on your device, deleting the app removes all of it — entries, photos and settings alike. There is no server-side copy for us to retain or delete.

Children

HobbyGo! is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with a new effective date.
