Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

Innerleaf ("the app", "we", "us") lets you scan the pages of your physical notebooks, flip through them like a real book, and jot down what you want to add next time you have the notebook in hand. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information.

The short version

Innerleaf does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data. There is no account, no login, and no server. Your notebooks, scans and notes stay on your device.

What's stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, inside iOS's app container:

  • Your notebooks — title, cover color, an optional cover photo, and when you created them.
  • Your pages — scanned page images, their order, and any written pages (title and body text) you typed instead of scanning.
  • Your "to add" notes — the text, whether it's done, and which notebook or page it belongs to.

Scans and cover photos are stored in the app's own private storage on your device. This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is not backed up to any server we control.

Camera

Innerleaf asks for camera access so you can scan notebook pages using Apple's built-in document scanner. Scanning happens entirely on your device: the images are saved straight into your notebook and are never uploaded. The camera is used only while you're actively scanning.

Photos

You can import an existing picture — as a page or as a notebook cover — through iOS's photo picker. The app only ever receives the image you pick; it has no access to the rest of your photo library.

Network use

Innerleaf makes no network requests. It works entirely offline.

What we do not do

  • We do not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or the Advertising Identifier (IDFA).
  • We include no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • We do not perform any cloud text recognition or send your scans anywhere.
  • We do not run a backend, and we do not sell or share your information with anyone.

Data retention and deletion

Deleting a notebook removes its pages and notes from your device. Deleting the app removes everything. There is no server-side copy for us to retain or delete.

Children

Innerleaf is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with a new effective date.
