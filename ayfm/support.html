Support

AYFM keeps nagging you to answer a message until you've actually replied. This page covers how it works and how to get help.

Contact

Email kud3t4@proton.me for bug reports, feature requests, or anything else. We read every message and try to reply within a few days.

How it works

  • Flag a message you need to reply to, and AYFM reminds you on a repeating schedule.
  • A reminder keeps nagging until you mark it answered — swipe a reminder to the right in the app, or use "Mark as Answered" on the notification.
  • You control how often each app nags you under Settings → default interval and app-specific intervals.

Flagging a message

You can create a reminder three ways:

  • In the app — tap the + button on the home screen and fill in the details.
  • The widget — add the AYFM "Quick Flag" widget to your Home Screen and tap an app's icon to start a reminder.
  • The share sheet — share a link or message to AYFM from another app.

Auto-flagging iMessage and Mail

iOS only allows automatic flagging for Apple's own apps — iMessage and Mail. You set this up once per contact in the Shortcuts app:

  • Shortcuts → Automation → + → choose the Message (or Email) automation.
  • Pick the sender, choose Run Immediately, then Next.
  • Add a New Blank Automation, scroll to AYFM, choose Automate AYFM, and fill in the "From who?" field.
  • Repeat for each contact you want auto-flagged.

Full step-by-step instructions are in the app under Settings → set it up. Every other app you flag yourself using the methods above.

Frequently asked questions

A reminder didn't nag me — why?

Make sure notifications are allowed for AYFM in iOS Settings → Notifications → AYFM, that you haven't paused reminders inside the app, and that a Focus or Do Not Disturb mode isn't silencing it — see below.

Does AYFM respect Focus and Do Not Disturb?

Yes. Reminders are delivered as ordinary notifications, so any Focus or Do Not Disturb mode silences them like anything else, and they're waiting for you in Notification Center when you come out of it. If you want AYFM to reach you during a Focus, add it as an allowed app in iOS Settings → Focus — that's iOS's own control, and it applies to every app the same way.

I'm getting a Shortcuts banner when my automation runs.

That brief "ran" banner is required by iOS for any automation set to "Run Immediately" and can't be turned off. It's separate from AYFM's own reminders.

I have a lot of open reminders — will some stop firing?

iOS limits an app to 64 scheduled notifications. When you're over the limit, AYFM gives the most overdue reminders their own individual nags and folds the rest into a single summary notification, so nothing goes silent.

How do I remove all my data?

Delete the app. All data is stored on your device, so removing the app removes everything.
